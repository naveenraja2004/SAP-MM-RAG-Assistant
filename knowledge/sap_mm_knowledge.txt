1. SAP MM BASICS
==================================================

SAP = Systems, Applications and Products in Data Processing.

MM = Materials Management.

SAP MM manages procurement, purchasing, inventory, material master data,
goods movements, invoice verification and material-related processes.

Main SAP MM areas:
- Material Master
- Vendor / Business Partner
- Purchasing
- Purchase Requisition
- Request for Quotation
- Quotation
- Purchase Order
- Goods Receipt
- Goods Issue

- Source List
- Purchasing Info Record
- Outline Agreement
- Scheduling Agreement
- Stock Management
- Valuation

- Stock Transport Order

3. MATERIAL MASTER
==================================================

Material Master contains important information about a material.

Important transactions:
MM01 - Create Material
MM02 - Change Material
MM03 - Display Material
MM06 - Flag Material for Deletion

Common Material Master views:
- Basic Data
- Purchasing
- MRP
- Accounting
- Plant/Storage
- Sales
- Quality Management

Common material types:
ROH - Raw Material
HALB - Semi-Finished Material
FERT - Finished Product
HAWA - Trading Goods
VERP - Packaging Material

Material number can be internal or external depending on configuration.

Common issue:
Material cannot be created.

Possible causes:
- Required organizational level not entered
- Material type configuration issue
- Number range issue
- Required field missing
- Authorization problem

Troubleshooting:
Check the material type, industry sector, organizational levels,
mandatory fields and user authorization.

21. AUTOMATIC ACCOUNT DETERMINATION
==================================================

Automatic Account Determination determines which G/L accounts are
posted during inventory transactions.

Important configuration transaction:
OBYC

Important concepts:
- Valuation Class
- Transaction Key
- Chart of Accounts
- Valuation Area

Common transaction keys:
BSX - Inventory Posting
WRX - GR/IR Clearing
GBB - Offsetting Entry for Inventory Posting
PRD - Price Difference

Common error:
"Account determination for entry ... not possible."

Troubleshooting:
1. Check material valuation class.
2. Check valuation area.
3. Check movement type.
4. Check OBYC configuration.
5. Check relevant transaction key.
6. Check G/L account assignment.
7. Verify chart of accounts.

Do not change production configuration without proper authorization
and testing.

22. GR/IR CLEARING
==================================================

GR/IR is a clearing account used between Goods Receipt and Invoice
Receipt.

GR:
Inventory / GR-IR postings may occur.

IR:
GR/IR is cleared against supplier liability depending on configuration.

Common issue:
GR/IR balance remains open.

Possible causes:
- GR quantity differs from invoice quantity
- Invoice not received
- Incorrect posting
- Reversal
- Price difference

38. PRACTICAL SAP MM TROUBLESHOOTING
==================================================

Q: What should I check if MIGO gives an error?
A: Check the material, plant, storage location, movement type, quantity,
posting date, stock availability, batch/serial requirements, purchase order
and user authorization. The exact error message should be checked before
making configuration changes.

Q: What should I check if MIRO gives an invoice error?
A: Check the purchase order, goods receipt, invoice quantity, invoice amount,
tax information, vendor/business partner, payment terms and invoice
verification settings. Also check whether the invoice is blocked.

Q: What should I check if a Purchase Order cannot be created?
A: Check vendor/business partner, material, plant, purchasing organization,
purchasing group, company code, document type, required master-data views,
source determination and user authorization.

Q: Why can a Purchase Order show an account assignment error?
A: The account assignment category may require additional information such
as cost center, internal order, asset or another account assignment object.
Enter the required account assignment data and verify the related
configuration.

Q: What should I check when automatic account determination fails?
A: Check the chart of accounts, valuation area, valuation class, movement
type, transaction/event key and OBYC configuration. The exact error message
should be analyzed before changing configuration.

Q: What should I check when material stock is not visible in MMBE?
A: Check the material number, plant, storage location, batch, stock type and
whether the relevant goods movement was posted successfully.

Q: What should I check when a material cannot be used in purchasing?
A: Check whether the material master contains the required Purchasing view
and organizational data such as plant. Also verify material status,
purchasing data and authorization.

Q: Why can Goods Receipt quantity differ from Purchase Order quantity?
A: Partial delivery, over-delivery tolerance, under-delivery tolerance,
delivery schedules or manual quantity entry can cause differences.
Company configuration determines which differences are permitted.

Q: What is an invoice block?
A: An invoice block prevents or restricts invoice processing or payment when
defined checks fail, such as quantity variance, price variance or other
invoice verification conditions.

Q: What should I check if a source is not determined for a material?
A: Check the source list, purchasing info record, outline agreements,
validity dates, source determination settings and whether the source is
relevant for automatic determination.

        ->
Source Determination
        ->
RFQ / Quotation
        ->
Purchase Order
        ->
Goods Receipt
        ->

A company needs 100 laptops.

==================================================

Q: What is SAP MM?
A: SAP MM is the SAP module that supports procurement, inventory
management, material-related master data and related purchasing processes.

Q: What is a Purchase Requisition?
A: An internal request for purchasing a material or service.

Q: What is a Purchase Order?
A: A formal purchasing document issued to a supplier.

Q: What is Goods Receipt?
A: A posting that records the receipt of material.

Q: What is Invoice Verification?
A: The process of checking supplier invoices against relevant purchasing and
goods receipt information.

Q: What is a Movement Type?
A: A code that controls the processing of a material movement.

Q: What is OBYC?
A: A configuration area used for automatic account determination.

Q: What is a Source List?
A: A record that defines permitted or relevant sources of supply for a
material and plant.

Q: What is a Purchasing Info Record?
A: It stores purchasing-related information for a material and supplier.

Q: What is an Outline Agreement?
A: A long-term purchasing agreement between an organization and supplier.

Q: What is a Scheduling Agreement?
A: A purchasing agreement containing delivery schedules for agreed quantities
over time.

Q: What is STO?
A: Stock Transport Order is used to plan and control movement of materials
between plants or locations according to the configured process.
